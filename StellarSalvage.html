HOW TO PLAY

Tap / click the asteroid → +scrap
Drones auto-mine while you idle
Frigates refine 100 scrap → 1 alloy
Foundries refine 50 alloy → 1 plasma
Warp Drive at 10K scrap → permanent ×1.05 multiplier

Tap multiplier upgrades grow your tap value. Drone/frigate/foundry levels stack. Modules give passive boosts that persist across warps.
